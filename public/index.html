A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we
get making.

Can you help if I don’t have finished artwork?+

Yes. Start with your logo, a reference or a description of your idea. We’ll review what you have and include any artwork preparation or design work in your quote. Vector files such as SVG, AI or EPS are ideal when available.

Do you make one-off pieces or only batches?+

Both. We can quote for one bespoke sign or a coordinated set. For labels, table markers and repeat components, share the full quantity so we can plan efficient production.

Can you make larger or outdoor signs?+

Tell us the dimensions, location and how the sign will be fitted. We’ll confirm what can be produced in-house and whether specialist fabrication or installation is needed. Outdoor use is assessed for material suitability and fixing requirements.

How much will my project cost?+

Every quote is based on size, materials, artwork, quantities and finishing. Share an approximate budget if you have one and we’ll help you explore an appropriate option. Orders are handled by quote and invoice.

How long does production take?+

Lead times depend on the brief, materials and current workload. We’ll confirm a timescale with your quote. If you have an opening date or event, include it in your brief so we can check availability.

Can I reorder the same design later?+

Yes. Quote your previous job reference and tell us the new quantity or any changes. We’ll confirm the current material availability, price and lead time before producing another batch.
